How to get funds off Blast5 steps

  1. Gather small balances first. Every withdrawal costs the same on Ethereum, whatever the amount. If you have several wallets with a little ETH each, send it to one wallet on Blast first; a transfer on Blast costs a tiny fraction of a cent.

  2. Turn tokens into ETH if you want ETH. WETH must be unwrapped to ETH before it can be withdrawn. USDB withdrawn through the bridge arrives on Ethereum as DAI and needs one more claim transaction, so swapping it to ETH on a Blast exchange first is often simpler.

  3. Start the withdrawal on blast.io/en/bridge, the official bridge, until 26 October. This needs a little ETH on Blast for gas.

  4. Prove it on Ethereum about an hour later. This is the first Ethereum transaction and needs ETH on Ethereum for gas. Check your address above and press Prove on the withdrawal.

  5. Claim it 24 hours after proving. Press Claim. This second Ethereum transaction releases the funds to the same address on Ethereum. USDB arrives as DAI after one more claim, which only the receiving wallet can send.

The Prove and Claim buttons call Blast's contracts on Ethereum directly, so they keep working after the bridge page closes. Any wallet can pay for them: a friend can prove or claim your withdrawal, and the funds still go only to the address that withdrew. Blast's guide: docs.blast.io/withdrawals/withdraw-to-ethereum. Blast PWA users: paste your PWA wallet address above to check it.

How the numbers workfees, prices, deposits, NFTs
  • Fees: one withdrawal needs two Ethereum transactions. Proving takes about 430,000 gas and claiming about 240,000 gas, the medians of recent transactions on the Blast portal contract. The page multiplies these by the current Ethereum gas price.
  • Worth it? "Yes" means the value is higher than the Ethereum fees still to pay. Ethereum gas changes by the minute, so a "No" can turn into a "Yes" when gas is cheaper.
  • Prices: ETH, USDB and BLAST from CoinGecko; other tokens from DexScreener, and only if a Blast pool holds at least $1,000 of liquidity.
  • Deposits in apps: lending receipts (Aave-style) count 1:1 with the token they hold, vault shares (ERC-4626) are converted by the vault itself, and v2 liquidity tokens by their share of the pool. Concentrated-liquidity positions are listed without a value.
  • NFTs cannot go through the official bridge. This page lists them but cannot see offers; a floor price is only the cheapest listing.
  • ETH on Ethereum is what this address holds there to pay for prove and claim.
  • Spam tokens and NFTs that scammers airdropped are counted but hidden. Never interact with them or visit sites named in them.
  • Withdrawal history covers the address's latest 100 transactions on Blast.
What this page readsno server, no tracking

Everything runs in your browser. There is no server and no tracking. Your address goes only to these public services:

  • rpc.blast.io, blast-rpc.publicnode.com: Blast balances and transactions
  • ethereum-rpc.publicnode.com (fallbacks 1rpc.io, cloudflare-eth.com): withdrawal status on Ethereum, gas price
  • api.routescan.io: which tokens and NFTs an address holds, transaction history
  • api.coingecko.com, api.dexscreener.com: prices (no address sent)

Checking makes only read requests (balances, contract reads, receipts). Open your browser's developer tools, Network tab, to watch every request, or view the page source.

Prove / Claim buttons (optional). Only when you press one, the page loads vendor/ethers-6.13.4.umd.min.js from this same site (an unmodified copy of ethers.js, MIT licence, checked by its hash), builds the transaction from Blast and Ethereum data, simulates it, and asks your wallet to send it. You confirm it in your wallet. The page never asks for approvals, message signatures, a seed phrase or a key, and never receives your funds: where the money goes is fixed inside the withdrawal.

Unofficial community tool. Not affiliated with Blast. It cannot move funds by itself: Prove and Claim only ask your own wallet to send a transaction to Blast's contracts, and you confirm it there. Check results on blastscan.io before acting, and withdraw only through the official bridge or Blast's documented contracts.
